Photo Stamp

Take a photo (or upload one), and it gets turned into a rough pencil sketch, cropped into a rubber-stamp seal with your name curved around the rim. Export it as a transparent PNG.

upload a photo, or take one on your phone
start by choosing or taking a photo above

Everything happens in your browser — the photo never leaves your device. The sketch is a Sobel edge-detection pass (grayscale → gradient → threshold), the seal border is a fixed 26-point scalloped die-cut shape, and the ring/rim text are redrawn live as you type.
