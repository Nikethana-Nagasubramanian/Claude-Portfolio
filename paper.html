My path here

My path here has been a little unusual. I started my career in SRE, moved closer to frontend engineering, then into product design — and over the last couple of years I've increasingly been bringing those disciplines back together.

I design products, build React/TypeScript interfaces, prototype with code and agents, and still carry some of the systems and performance thinking I learned as an SRE.

That's actually why I'm writing.

Would Paper consider an 8–12 week paid design-engineering residency?

Not a ceremonial internship where I shadow the team. Give me actual work.
Let me design, build, fix things, take feedback, and ship alongside you.

I think I could contribute immediately in a few places:

  • Product design + implementation — I can take an interface from problem → interaction → working frontend.
  • Rapid coded prototyping — I've increasingly been using agents as part of my design/build workflow rather than treating design and engineering as separate phases.
  • Product thinking — I've worked on 0→1 products and I'm comfortable with ambiguity, user research, and figuring out what actually needs to be built.
  • Performance + systems thinking — I started in SRE, so performance and reliability aren't abstract engineering concerns to me. That foundation still influences how I think about the interfaces and frontend systems I build.

What I want:
The opportunity to work alongside people with an unusually high bar for interface craft and become better by shipping real work with them.
